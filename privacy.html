Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

FinRise Studio Automation is a personal tool used by the channel owner to create and upload educational videos to the FinRise Studio YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube Data

The tool uses Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data API to authorize video uploads to the channel owner's YouTube account.

It uses the YouTube upload permission solely to upload videos and their associated metadata. It does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Storage and Security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored privately and are not intentionally published. Video generation and upload data may be processed by the services used to operate the automation.

Data Retention

The channel owner may delete locally or privately stored automation data and revoke Google account access through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the FinRise Studio channel owner through the YouTube channel.
